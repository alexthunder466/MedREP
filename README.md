# 💊 MedREP - Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutic Intelligence Portal

[![Streamlit App](https://static.streamlit.io/badges/streamlit_badge_black_white.svg)](https://medrep.streamlit.app/)

*MedREP* is an advanced clinical pharmacology and therapeutic intelligence platform designed to bridge the gap between medical data and patient treatment. By integrating international pharmacopeias (USP, BP, EP, and WHO guidelines), MedREP provides healthcare professionals, medical representatives, and patients with instant, reliable access to therapeutic insights and drug protocols.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Universal Monograph Search:** Dynamically fetches drug monographs across USP, BP, EP, and WHO registries using RxNav and OpenFDA integration.
* **Smart Dosage-Form Visualizer:** Automatically pairs drug formulations (tablets, injections, capsules, syrups, inhalers) with high-quality visual context.
* **Global Pharmacopeia Cross-Mapping:** Automatically reconciles international nonproprietary names and drug synonyms (e.g., Paracetamol ↔ Acetaminophen, Salbutamol ↔ Albuterol).
* **Live Regulatory & Medical Alerts:** Integrated real-time RSS feed delivering the latest FDA approvals, public health warnings, and voluntary drug recalls in an interactive news carousel.
* **Clinical Protocol Database:** Detailed clinical management protocols for common conditions like Pediatric Malaria, Essential Hypertension, Type 2 Diabetes, and Community-Acquired Pneumonia.
